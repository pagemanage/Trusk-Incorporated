Core Philosophy

  • Musical exploration, not chaotic
  • Maximum simultaneous module usage
  • Continuous signal chains throughout
  • Tascam as Performance Command Center
  • Syntakt + Tascam = 22-track USB merge
  • Passive T-Network splits are valid and encouraged
  • Pico DSP lives inside Magneto feedback loop

Fixed Architecture Decisions

  • Acid Source → Syntakt Ext In L
  • Plonk → Syntakt Ext In R
  • Quad VCA = modulation only (not audio)
  • Triple Steeple = 3-ch envelope generator
  • T-Networks 103/104 = analog percussion voices
  • Tascam AUX 2 = disabled for internal FX
  • DXG blends Ensemble OSC + Interstellar Radio

Tascam Model 12 Topology Reminder: Channels 1–6 are mono with preamps. Ch 7/8 and 9/10 are stereo pairs. Inserts only on channels 1 and 2. Two aux buses — AUX 2 is disabled for internal FX. Syntakt + Tascam merged = 22 simultaneous tracks in DAW.

Ch Source Module Orchestral Role Insert / Processing Notes
1 Acid Source Bass / Double Bass Cosmotronic Messor INSERT Also routed to Syntakt Ext In L simultaneously

4 Doepfer QZVCO Trombone — 4 oscillators available; can use individual outs for wider image

Tascam AUX Bus Usage

AUX 1 — Magneto Send

  • Any channel can be sent to AUX 1
  • AUX 1 Out → Strymon Magneto Input
  • Magneto Wet Out → Tascam Ch 7/8
  • Use pre-fader sends for parallel processing
  • Feedback depth controlled by Magneto repeat knob

AUX 2 — Disabled

  • AUX 2 is reserved / disabled
  • Not available for external routing
  • Internal FX bus only (bypassed)
  • Do not patch into AUX 2 send/return

Bastl Pizza → MISO Ch A + Acid Source → MISO Out → Syntakt Ext L

Philosophy: Don't just fade volume — fade Energy. As Eurorack oscillators rise on faders 1–6, they push against VST orchestras via Messor compression (4:1 ratio). The orchestra naturally ducks to make room. Transitions feel intentional rather than chaotic.

Live Performance Tips
Tascam EQ Strategy
Keep flat for improvisation. Use HPF on channels 1–4 to prevent low-end mud under the Acid Source bass. Use the Tascam's channel EQ only for corrective cuts, not creative shaping — leave creative EQ to the rack modules themselves.
DXG as Performance Tool
The crossfader between Ensemble OSC and Interstellar Radio is your "sanity-to-chaos" dial. Sweeping it live during a take transitions the string section from lush and tonal to gritty and unpredictable — one physical gesture, maximum musical impact.
